The Midwest Machining Case File

You have been assigned to investigate Midwest Machining LLC, a heavy industrial manufacturing firm. We have extracted their raw system data into a single, multi-sheet Excel file. It contains seeded anomalies hidden among thousands of normal transactions.

  • 3 Worksheets
  • 2,500+ Rows
  • 4 Critical Anomalies

Power BI Build Lab

Once you have downloaded the Excel file, open Power BI Desktop. Click Get Data -> Excel Workbook, select your downloaded file, and check all three sheets to load them. Complete the missions below to uncover the truth.

Mission 01

Useful Life Manipulation

Your Objective

Visualize the relationship between the Acquisition Date of heavy machinery and its assigned Useful Life. Your goal is to construct a visual that highlights any sudden, unnatural changes in corporate accounting policies over time.

Target Data: Fixed Assets Sheet

Create a New Page in your Power BI report and name it 'Blueprint 01'.
Click on the canvas and insert a Line and Clustered Column Chart visual.
In the Data Pane, drag Acquisition_Date to the X-axis field. Remove Quarter and Day so it only shows Year and Month.
Drag Asset_ID to the Column Y-axis field. (Ensure it aggregates as 'Count').
Drag Useful_Life_Months to the Line Y-axis field. Click the drop-down on the field and change the aggregation from 'Sum' to 'Average'.
Open the Format Pane (the paint roller icon). Go to Lines -> Stroke Width and increase it to 4px to make the manipulation obvious.

"Watch the line. The average useful life of heavy shop floor machinery suddenly spikes from 60 to 120 months in 2024. They are stretching depreciation to artificially boost their bottom line."

Interpretation Check

What does the sudden spike in the useful life trend line likely indicate?

Mission 02

Threshold Abuse (Invoice Splitting)

Your Objective

Midwest's capitalization policy dictates that purchases strictly OVER $5,000 require corporate approval. Using the capitalized amounts, create a visualization that proves plant managers are deliberately splitting invoices to stay under the radar.

Target Data: Fixed Assets Sheet

Create a New Page in your Power BI report and name it 'Blueprint 02'.
In the Data Pane, right-click on the Capitalized_Amount column and select New Group.
In the grouping window, set the Group Type to Bin, and set the Bin Size to 100. Click OK.
Add a Clustered Column Chart to the canvas.
Drag your newly created Capitalized_Amount (bins) to the X-axis.
Drag Asset_ID to the Y-axis (Set to 'Count').
Go to the Analytics Pane (magnifying glass icon). Select X-axis Constant Line, click 'Add', set the value to 5000, and color the line bright red.

"Notice the massive, unnatural spike in invoice counts right at the $4,900 bin. Statistical averages would show a smooth bell curve, but human behavior overrides statistics. Plant managers are splitting invoices to avoid corporate oversight."

Interpretation Check

Why is there a massive spike in transactions just below the $5,000 mark?

Mission 03

Ghost Margins

Your Objective

You need to analyze thousands of inventory items at once. Find a way to visually isolate specific parts that are both highly obsolete (very old) AND suspiciously held at a very high total value.

Target Data: Inventory Sheet

Create a New Page in your Power BI report and name it 'Blueprint 03'.
In the Home ribbon, click New Measure.
Type this exactly into the formula bar to calculate the total financial value of each item on hand:
Total_Value = SUMX('Inventory', 'Inventory'[Quantity_On_Hand] * 'Inventory'[Unit_Cost])
Add a Scatter Plot visual to the canvas.
Drag Days_In_Inventory to the X-Axis. Drag your new Total_Value measure to the Y-Axis.
Drag SKU to Values to plot individual parts as distinct dots.

"Look at the data points isolated far into the top-right quadrant. You have heavy machinery parts sitting there for over 800 days, yet valued at hundreds of thousands of dollars. They are obsolete 'Ghost Inventory', but Midwest is refusing to write them off to protect asset values."

Interpretation Check

In this specific scatter plot, what do the data points isolated in the top-right quadrant represent?

Mission 04

Rogue Entries

Your Objective

Map over 10,000 journal entries across the days of the week and hours of the day. Identify a way to instantly filter the data and highlight unusual, high-dollar posting behaviors by executives.

Target Data: Journal Entries Sheet

Create a New Page in your Power BI report and name it 'Blueprint 04'.
Add a Matrix Visual to the canvas.
Drag Day_of_Week to Rows.
Drag Hour_of_Day to Columns.
Drag Entry_Amount to Values.
Go to Format your visual, select Cell elements, apply setting to Sum of Entry_Amount, turn on Background color, and click the fx button to apply the color setting (set lowest value to light gray and highest to bright red).
Add a Slicer visual and drag User_Role into it. Click the "CFO" role to filter the matrix.

"When you filter the matrix by the CFO, the table goes entirely grey—except for a massive bright red cluster late on Saturday nights at the exact end of every quarter. This is a textbook management override of internal controls to manipulate financial reporting."

Interpretation Check

What does the bright red cluster of massive journal entries posted by the CFO on a Saturday at 11:00 PM strongly suggest?
