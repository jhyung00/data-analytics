Pivot Tables & Filtering

REQ 2

Distributions & Outliers

Navigate to the 'Daily_Sales' tab. You will analyze the distribution of daily revenue.

1. Use Data -> Data Analysis -> Histogram to create a histogram of the Daily Revenue. Note: First, you must create a manual 'Bins' column (e.g., in column E) starting from 1,000 to 8,000 in increments of 1,000. Based on the visual output, is the data positively or negatively skewed?

2. Calculate the Z-score for every daily revenue value in the blank 'Z-Score' column (Column C). Using the standard rule (Absolute Z-Score > 3), how many outliers are present in the dataset?

Hint: Use Data -> Data Analysis -> Descriptive Statistics (check 'Summary statistics') to find the Mean and Standard Deviation. Then, calculate the Z-scores in Column C using those summary values and use COUNTIFS to find outliers.

You must construct the SUMPRODUCT formulas yourself in the blank colored cells for 'Assembly Hours Used', 'Testing Hours Used', and 'Total Profit' before running Solver.

  • Objective (Green Cell): Maximize Total Profit (Cell E15).
  • Changing Cells (Yellow Cells): Units Produced for 'Alpha' and 'Beta' (Cells C5:D5).

    • Testing Hours Used (E9) <= Available Limit (G9)
    • Units Produced must be Integers.

REQ 5

Optimization II: Advertising Campaign

Navigate to the 'Ad_Mix_Solver' tab. Determine the optimal number of ads to purchase across platforms to maximize total audience reach.

Formulas Required

Like before, the red-outlined calculation cells are blank! Construct the appropriate SUMPRODUCT formulas for 'Total Spent' and 'Total Reach' before configuring Solver.

  • Objective (Green): Maximize Total Reach (Cell D16).
  • Changing Cells (Yellow): Ads Purchased per platform (D5:D7).
  • Constraints (Orange):
    • Total Spent (D10) <= Max Budget (D11).
    • Social Ads Purchased (D5) >= Min Social Ads (D12).
    • TV Ads Purchased (D6) <= Max TV Ads (D13).
    • Ads Purchased (D5:D7) must be integers.

1. Solve the media mix model. What is the maximum Total Reach achieved within the budget?

2. In the optimal solution, how many TV Ads are purchased?
